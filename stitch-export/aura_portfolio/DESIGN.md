## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, high-precision aesthetic tailored for elite creatives, architects, engineers, and digital executives. It reflects uncompromising clarity, intellectual rigor, and modern elegance. 

- **Aesthetic Direction:** Elevated Modern Minimalism mixed with subtle Glassmorphic and Tonal accents. High-contrast typography paired with luminous electric accents creates an immediate sense of authority and modern craftsmanship.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires trust, technological polish, creative distinction, and calm confidence.
- **Target Audience:** Design leaders, technical innovators, prospective clients, and high-value hiring partners who evaluate craft down to the pixel.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around crisp contrast, crystalline clarity, and focused vibrancy:

- **Primary (`#1d4ed8` — Cobalt/Electric Blue):** Serves as the high-impact accent for primary calls-to-action, interactive highlights, active indicators, and critical focus states.
- **Secondary (`#38bdf8` — Luminous Sky Blue):** Used for ethereal tints, micro-badges, pill highlights, gentle glow gradients, and delicate interactive hover surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#0f172a` — Deep Slate Midnight):** The foundational anchor for high-contrast headlines, deep ink body copy, and structural anchors.
- **Neutral (`#64748b` — Slate):** Manages secondary descriptions, timestamps, subheadings, and subtle UI lines across light surfaces.
- **Background & Canvas (`#ffffff` and `#f8fafc`):** Pristine white base with cool slate-tinted canvas containers to yield clean negative space without clinical sterility.

## Typography

The typographic pairing marries structural geometric precision with warm, highly readable human ergonomics:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides an architectural, contemporary cadence. Tight negative tracking on large scales accentuates confident modernity.
- **Body & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Offers open counters and friendly clarity, ensuring case studies, biography text, and technical project manifests remain effortless to read.
- **Rhythm & Hierarchy:** Strict proportional leading ensures vertical harmony. Headings and section titles use uppercase kickers set in `label-sm` with letter-spacing tracking to frame primary thoughts.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8pt spatial baseline underpins all composition and card structures:

- **Grid Strategy:** A fluid 12-column grid system for desktop screen sizes (`>1024px`) reflowing into a 6-column grid on tablet devices (`768px-1023px`), and a unified single/double column structure on mobile (`<767px`).
- **Mobile First Optimization:** Margins tighten dynamically to `1.25rem` on mobile, safeguarding precious horizontal viewport space while preserving generous vertical padding between sections to allow content breathing room.
- **Max Canvas Width:** Portfolio contents sit in a constrained maximum width of `1200px` for optimal reading tracking and image containment.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: low-contrast borders combined with ultra-diffused ambient illumination.

- **Base Level (Level 0):** Canvas backdrop (`#f8fafc` or `#ffffff`).
- **Card Rest State (Level 1):** Solid white surface bordered with 1px hairline stroke (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`), combined with a subtle shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Card Interactive / Hover (Level 2):** Elevated via `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and border shift to `rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.25)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Sticky Navbars (Level 3):** Glassmorphic translucent surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) coupled with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and perimeter stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces modern curvature (Level 2), projecting a welcoming yet polished personality:

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, inline badges, and buttons.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Project preview cards, experience blocks, and gallery items.
- **Hero Containers & Modals (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Major featured work displays and prominent layout wrappers.
- **Micro UI & Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter tags, avatar frames, and status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep cobalt background (`#1d4ed8`), white text, `rounded-md` (8px), subtle inset highlight, smooth hover transition shifting background to `#1e40af` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Translucent slate background (`rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.7)`), deep slate text (`#0f172a`), 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Hover triggers full white background with border `#38bdf8`.
- **Ghost:** Pure text with an interactive underline reveal and cobalt arrow slide effect.

### Cards (Portfolio & Case Studies)
- Pure white container, `rounded-lg` border radius, encased in a 1px soft slate border (`#e2e8f0`).
- Features a generous internal padding (`space-lg`), high-fidelity aspect-ratio image header with rounded top edges, followed by title, tech stack pill list, and impact metrics.
- Card transitions upward by `4px` on hover with electric blue border illumination.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Availability Chip:** Compact pill shape with a pulsing neon emerald or sky blue live dot, gentle sky blue background (`#f0f9ff`), and primary cobalt text (`#0369a1`).
- **Skill / Tag Chips:** Neutral slate fill (`#f1f5f9`), slate text (`#475569`), hover state transitions to subtle cobalt tint.

### Inputs & Contact Fields
- Base height of 48px for comfortable mobile touch targets.
- 1px neutral slate border (`#cbd5e1`) on white base; transitions to an active cobalt ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`) and border `#1d4ed8` on focus.

### Interactive Metrics / Stat Callouts
- Large tabular numerals rendered in `Space Grotesk` with an electric blue gradient mask, paired with micro-labels in slate below.
